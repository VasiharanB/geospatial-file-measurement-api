ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

Authoritative Processing Pipeline

Zero-compromise GIS calculation stack designed for enterprise reliability, security, and precision.

01

Hardened Ingestion

Magic byte verification, XML entity (XXE) and SSRF defense, Zip-Slip path containment, and 100 MB decompression bomb bounds.

02

Automated CRS Resolution

Calculates geometry centroids and automatically determines local metric UTM zones or World Cylindrical Equal Area projections.

03

Planar Metric Engine

PyProj and Shapely 2.0 execute coordinate transformation and calculate metric planar area (m²) and length (m) with auto geometry repair.

04

Relational Persistence

SQLite in WAL mode managed via SQLAlchemy 2.0 with foreign keys, cascading deletion, and atomic worker thread execution.
